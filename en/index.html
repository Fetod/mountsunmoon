Our Positioning

One box, five crafts

The coffee market is crowded. Most brands compete on origin, on light versus dark roast, on value for money.

Mount Sun Moon does not chase a single style. It is a curated platform. Good coffee comes in more than one form, and every roaster has their own fire and their own taste. So rather than one brand declaring one standard, we invited five artisans with very different lives behind them to interpret the land in their own way. Mount Sun Moon then selects, arranges and places their work in one box.

One box of drip-bag coffee, five flavours, laid out like a map. All you need is hot water and a cup to get to know five artisans, one at a time, and find the one you love. Together the five flavours stand for balance across the five elements: metal, wood, water, fire and earth.

Our Story

Heaven, earth and people in a name

Sun, moon, mountain: three characters that paint a picture of heaven, earth and people.

天

Heaven · Sun and moon

The fullest light by day, the soft moon by night, and the stars farther still. Light comes from above; it makes all things grow and gives time its rhythm.

地

Earth · The mountain

The mountain never speaks, yet it holds everything, and it has watched the sun and moon rise and set countless times. Coffee trees on its slopes take in the land and the essence of sun and moon, and that is where the taste of every bean begins.

人

People · The artisans, and you with your cup

Five artisans turn the land into flavour through fire. You, drinking this cup, are the final brushstroke of the painting. Heaven and earth give light and nourishment; people settle their hearts and become light themselves.

In tune with heaven and earth

We respect the land and the seasons, and draw out nature as it is. The turning of sun and moon is where Mount Sun Moon begins.

One person, one flavour

Every roaster has a name, a story and a fire of their own. We want you to know the people who make the coffee, not just a bag of coffee.

Passing the light on

Sun, moon and stars shine quietly, watching over every life on Earth from across the vast universe: people, animals and plants, mountains, rivers and forests. Mount Sun Moon wants to pass that light on, to the people who drink our coffee and to children. Every pre-order gives 5% of the product price to arts and music programmes in experimental education.

Brand Spirit

Still at heart as the mountain,
radiant in celestial light.

山靜心安，不語亦有光。

"The benevolent delight in mountains," as the old saying goes. A mountain stands firm not because it escapes wind and rain, but because its roots run deep. The first thing Mount Sun Moon wants to say is about inner calm. Boil the water, wait, let the aroma rise, and sit down steady, like a mountain.

High on the mountain, coffee trees quietly receive the care of sun, moon and stars year after year, until they bear good beans. Steady as a mountain, quietly holding the light of sun and moon.

We want to take one more step. The light of sun and moon falls on us, but we carry light of our own too. Everyone shines. Not the brightness of a light bulb, but the warmth people feel around someone whose heart is settled and full of kindness and compassion, like a spring breeze. The mountain does not speak, yet it has light; a person need not speak to have light either.

So we hope this cup first helps you settle your heart, then lets you shine your own light, gently, like the sun, moon and stars, on the people around you and on the world.

Brewing yourself a cup every morning is a ritual, a taste, a set of values, and a way of living.

Still at heart · radiant without a word

The symbol in our logo

Two peaks, climbing high to see far

The mountain line has two peaks, one after another, rising as if to lead people onward, to climb high and see far. The colour of the mountain and the sun comes from the golden yellow of the Loess Plateau: on metal it becomes a flowing, shimmering gold; on other materials it turns a deep, slightly mottled yellow, like the natural roughness of the earth. The moon is a clear, bright white, which on metal becomes a silvery glow.

Beyond what we see, endless possibility

The large gold circle at the summit is the sun; the smaller white circle in front is the bright moon. Offset from each other, the moon covers part of the sun, so what you see looks like a golden crescent. What the eye sees is not always the whole picture. May we look at the world with a wider view, enjoy what is beautiful, and imagine endless possibilities.

Origin Notes

Origin notes: which mountain your cup comes from

The beans our five artisans chose come from the highlands of six countries on three continents. Get to know a bean's home, and you understand the taste in your cup a little better.

Kenya

Kirinyaga

Approx. 1,600–1,900 m above sea level

On the southern slopes of Mount Kenya, red volcanic soil gives a bright acidity. Kenya excels at washed processing, often with blackcurrant and berry notes. AA is the top grade, based on bean size.

Emily

Indonesia

Sumatra · Mandheling

North Sumatra

Mandheling takes its name from the Mandailing people of North Sumatra. Beans here are often wet-hulled, giving a heavy body with cocoa and herbal notes. Golden Mandheling is a select grade, hand-sorted several more times.

Warren

Guatemala

Huehuetenango

Approx. 1,500–2,000 m above sea level

In the north-western highlands bordering Mexico, one of Guatemala's highest-growing regions. Hot, dry air from the Mexican plains keeps the high slopes frost-free, giving clean flavours and full sweetness.

WarrenBao Ge

Ethiopia

Yirgacheffe

Approx. 1,700–2,200 m above sea level

The birthplace of Arabica coffee. Yirgacheffe, in the southern highlands, is famous for florals and citrus; natural processing adds a ripe-fruit sweetness. G1 is Ethiopia's highest grade of green coffee.

Warren

Colombia

Supremo

The Andes

Supremo is not a region but a Colombian size grade, meaning large beans of screen 17 and above. The Andes run the length of the country, giving balanced flavour and round sweetness.

Warren

Honduras

La Paz

Montecillos range, approx. 1,400–1,550 m above sea level

An important coffee country in Central America whose specialty coffee has improved fast in recent years. Elias's family estate is in La Paz in the south-west, where smallholders and family farms often try experimental processes such as honey and anaerobic fermentation.

DaheElias

Altitudes are typical ranges for each region; actual figures vary by lot and estate.

How long does the coffee keep?

12 months unopened at room temperature. Store in a cool, dry place away from heat, humidity and direct sunlight. Brew soon after opening.

How do I brew it best?

Tap the bag so the grounds settle, then tear the filter open along the dotted line. Pull out the side hooks and hang the bag steadily over your cup. Pour a little hot water and let it bloom for 20 seconds, then pour in stages up to 150–180 ml. Lift out the filter, let it drain gently, and enjoy. Recommended water temperature: 88–92°C.
